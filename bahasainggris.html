Taipei 101, The Building of Technology

Mirkella Qualife Linardo 8G / 20

Taipei 101 is a tall building owned by the Taipei Financial Center Corporation. This tower was built in 2004, and it was declared the tallest building at the time. Taipei 101 has one of Taiwan's fastest elevators, reaching approximately 60.6 km/h. It is used as an office and a tourist site for foreigners as its height reaches 508m, which attracts a lot of visitors. Taipei 101 is a tall skyscraper with over 100 floors, not only does it have advanced technologies, but also a captivating scenery.

And Up We Go!

With over 100 floors, Taipei 101 stands tall above the city, requiring a specially designed elevator to transport visitors swiftly to the top. This elevator can take us up to floor 89 in just 45 seconds. Its speed reaches over 60.6km/h and makes your ears tingling after just one ride

What a Strong Tower?!

Using outstanding technology, Taipei 101 can withstand many natural disasters and harsh weather conditions. The technology they use is a tuned mass damper, also known as a TMD. This giant ball weighs as heavy as around 3.5 houses, which is approximately 660 tons of golden steel. This device acts as a pendulum to prevent natural disasters such as earthquakes and harsh wind conditions from possibly collapsing the building. This damper works by isolating the building from the earth, creating a relative shift that produces a counterforce, effectively reducing the energy of the earthquake.

Mesmerizing Views

From a very elevated viewpoint, you can see the whole magnificence of Taiwan from just 1 floor. The shape of the floor is like a donut, which makes it easier to look at Taiwan in its entirety. The view from above is majestic, allowing you to see everything in Taiwan. Not only that, in this tower, there is a specific spot where the floor is made of glass, making it transparent. This allows visitors to have a thrilling experience and take Vogue-worthy photos.

Not Just a Skyscraper

Taipei 101 is a tall tower with 101 floors, it not only has advanced technologies, but also captivating views. I truly recommend that you go to Taipei 101 to experience Taiwan at its fullest. In Taipei 101, I learned a lot of things like how Taiwan, a country full of earthquakes, deals with this problem. Taipei 101 is not just a skyscraper, it’s a symbol of Taiwan's prosperity.
